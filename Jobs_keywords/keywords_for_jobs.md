# HealthCareers Job Taxonomy — Titles & Skill Keywords

Two top-level **categories** — Non Clinical and Public Health — each with 10 **sub-categories**.

Matching rules:

- **Titles** are the high-precision signal: a title match classifies the job directly.
- **Strong skill keywords** are near-unique to one sub-category: 2+ co-occurring strong keywords can classify a job even without a title match.
- **Weak skill keywords** are supporting signal only — they break ties, never match alone.
- **Negative keywords** (bottom of this doc) veto a match regardless of tier.

## Cross-cutting negative keywords (veto list)

A match on any of these blocks classification regardless of tier:

| Negative keyword(s) | Protects against |
|---|---|
| medical billing, AR caller, AR calling, revenue cycle, RCM, collections, claims adjudication | Medical Coding false positives |
| mechanical & electrical, M&E engineer, MEP | Monitoring & Evaluation false positives |
| sales, business development, BD executive, telecalling | MSL / Clinical Research false positives |
| regulatory compliance (finance/banking/legal context) | Regulatory Affairs false positives |
| Indian Penal Code, legal | IPC false positives |
| rheumatoid arthritis | RA false positives |
| clinical dietitian, hospital dietitian | keeps Nutrition to program roles |
| staff nurse, lab technician, phlebotomist | keeps clinical roles out entirely |

## Implementation notes

1. **Short tokens (RA, TMF, IPC, CHO, MSL, CTA, CDM)** must only match in job titles as whole words — never in free-text skills or descriptions.
2. **MedDRA and causality assessment appear in three sub-categories** (Pharmacovigilance, Medical Reviewer, Clinical Data Management): tie-break by title first, then by count of strong-keyword hits per sub-category.
3. **Fold-in rules:** MCH, WASH, environmental health, mental health, emergency response, policy, and HSS roles map into Program Management or Research; health economics always tags as HEOR under Non Clinical.
